## Findings (most severe first)

N1 [note] src/bin/hmn/main.rs (Ps doc, shown in `hmn ps --help` and `hmn --help`): the sentence "every process the caller's sandbox lets it inspect is listed, and the rest are counted ... as unreadable" overstates. Readable processes that hold no graphics_footprint bytes are not listed. Evidence: `sandbox.sh S --job -- bash -c 'hmn ps; python3 count_denied.py'` printed a table of 2 rows (gpujob, hmn) and `denied=1054 read=3`. It is the spec's own wording (Step 2 item 5), so it is a spec issue. Fix: "every process holding ledger bytes that the sandbox lets it read is listed, and the processes it cannot read are counted".

N2 [note] Wiring with no cargo-test pin. Three mutations survive `cargo test --all-features` (bin 279 + cli_ps 4 + ignored 1) and are caught only by the CLI gates:
 - M1, `notes.unreadable += listing.denied_pids.len()` (unfiltered): all tests pass; the g16 gate turns `pid=4294967295 exit 1 unreadable=0` into `exit 2 unreadable=1`. Verified on a release build.

Spec-sanctioned (README GATE CONTRACT); recorded here because the strings `hmn: watch failed to query device D: ...` and the run_ps skip/--device lines have no whole-string cargo test of their own. The helper-level strings are whole-string pinned.

N3 [note] tests/cli_ps.rs: DENIAL_TEXT and NO_GPU_SOURCE_TEXT are hand-copied prefixes of `HypomnesisError::{ProcessListDenied, NoGpuSource}` Display. Nothing ties them to the lib, so a Display rewording would label a real macos-latest skip line `rejected` instead of failing a unit test. Fix: a test that asserts `HypomnesisError::NoGpuSource.to_string().starts_with(NO_GPU_SOURCE_TEXT)` and the same for the denial (the lib is a dependency of tests).
